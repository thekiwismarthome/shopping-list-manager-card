# Categories

Items are grouped by category so they match the order you walk through the shop. There are thirteen default categories: Fruit & Veg, Dairy & Eggs, Meat & Seafood, Bakery, Pantry and more. Each has its own colour.

## Filter by category

A row of chips sits under the summary. Swipe it sideways to see every category, or drag it with the mouse. Tap a chip to show only that category, and tap **All** to see everything again.

## Order

Each list keeps its own category order, so you can match the layout of your usual shop.

## Recently used

**Recently used** is not a category. It is a separate section under the categories, so frequently bought items are always one tap away.

::: info Managing categories
The **Manage Categories** page under Settings shows the categories. Adding and reordering them from there is still being built.
:::
