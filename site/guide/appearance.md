# Appearance

Open **Settings → Appearance** to change how the card looks. Settings are saved for your Home Assistant user and follow you between devices.

## Interface style

| Design | Look |
|---|---|
| **Fresh** (default) | Soft cards, a summary card with progress and an estimated total, category chips, a stepper on each row, a + button and a floating bottom bar. |
| **Compact** | Dense and list first. Checkbox rows, a progress line, collapsible categories and a flat tab bar with a total bar. |

You can compare them side by side in the [live demo](/demo).

## Theme

Three themes work with both designs:

| Theme | Accent |
|---|---|
| **Meadow** | Green. Fresh's default. |
| **Slate** | Periwinkle blue. Compact's default. |
| **Sunset** | Warm orange. |

A theme changes the accent colour (the summary card, buttons, progress, tick boxes and the active tab). The backgrounds stay as the design defines them.

## Colour mode

- **Light** and **Dark** are fixed.
- **Match device** follows your phone, computer or Home Assistant theme.

Fresh starts in light and Compact starts in dark until you choose.

## Display

- **Use emojis instead of icons** for categories and items.
- **Tiles per row** in tile view. **Auto** is the default: 3 on a phone, 4 on a folding phone that is opened out, and 5 on a tablet, following the width of the card. Pick a number from 2 upwards instead if you prefer. You can also change it from the **List options** when you are in tile view.
- **Recently used** on or off, and how many products to show.
- **Product images**, including a local folder in Home Assistant to match images by product name.

## Font

Each design has its own font. You can choose a different font family, and use your device's text size or set a size and weight yourself.
