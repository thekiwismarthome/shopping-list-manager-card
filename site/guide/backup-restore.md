# Backup and restore

Find this under **Settings → FAQ & Support → Backup & Restore**.

## Export

Choose **Download backup** to save a JSON file with your lists and the products you added yourself. Catalogue products are left out because they reload automatically.

## Import

Choose **Choose backup file** and pick a backup. Your existing data is kept. Only lists and products that are missing are added, so importing never overwrites what you have.

## When to back up

- Before you upgrade the integration or the card.
- Before you switch region.
- When you move to a new Home Assistant installation.

::: info Not included
The backup covers lists and your own products. Card appearance settings are saved with your Home Assistant user, so they do not need a backup.
:::
