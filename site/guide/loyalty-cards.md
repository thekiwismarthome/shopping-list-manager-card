# Loyalty cards

Keep your store cards in one place and show the barcode at the checkout without finding the plastic.

## Add a card

1. Open the **Loyalty** tab from the bottom bar.
2. Add a new card. You can scan the barcode with your phone's camera or type the number in.
3. Give the card a name. As you type, the main supermarkets for your region are suggested. Choose a colour and add any notes.
4. Add a **picture** for the store (see below), then save it.

Standard barcodes and QR codes are both supported.

## Add a picture of the store

Under **Store picture**, press **Choose picture** and pick a logo or photo from your phone. On most phones you can also take one with the camera. The picture is shrunk and tidied automatically, with any see-through areas turned white, and it appears on the card and in tile view. Whichever side of the picture is longer fits the space, so it is never cropped or stretched.

To use a picture from the web instead, paste its address under **or paste an image address**. Press **Remove** to take the picture off the card. You can change it later from the card's edit form.

The picture is saved on your Home Assistant as a small file. It is removed again when you delete the card, unless another card still uses it.

## Show a card at the checkout

Tap the card. A sheet slides up from the bottom with a large barcode or QR code on a white background, so scanners can read it. Tap outside the sheet, or swipe it down, to close it.

Each card shows the last four digits of its number, so you can tell similar cards apart without exposing the whole number.

## Edit or remove a card

Press the three dots on a card to change its name, colour or notes, or to delete it.

## Private and shared cards

A card can be visible to everyone in the household or kept private to you. Private cards can be shared with chosen people. Once [groups](groups) are set up, a new card starts in your household, and you can choose **Only me** instead. A card's menu has **Manage members** to share it with other households or people.

::: tip Phone brightness
Turn your screen brightness up before holding a barcode to a scanner. Some scanners struggle with dim screens.
:::
