# Items and quantities

## Add an item

Press the **+** button on the Shop tab (in Compact, use the search box at the top) and start typing. The catalogue suggests products as you type. Pick one to add it with its usual unit and price, or add what you typed as a new item.

Items you used recently appear in their own **Recently used** section under the categories, so a tap adds them again. You can switch that section off in **Settings → Appearance**.

## Tick items off

- **Fresh:** tap an item to tick it. It moves to the bottom of its category and shows **Undo**.
- **Compact:** tap the checkbox.

Ticked items stay on the list until you clear them. Open the list options and choose **Clear checked items** to remove them all at once.

## Change quantity

- **Fresh:** use the **−** and **+** buttons on the row.
- **Tile view:** tap a tile to add one, and tap the minus to take one away. Taking the quantity to zero removes the item.

Quantities can be in units, kilograms, grams, litres, millilitres, packs and more.

## Edit details

Press and hold an item to open its details. You can change the name, category, unit, price and add a note.

## Prices and totals

If a product has a price, the row shows the cost for the quantity you want, and the list shows an estimated total in your region's currency. You can hide prices in the list options.

## Search the catalogue

Search matches names and common alternatives, and copes with small spelling differences. You can filter out products containing an allergen, and ask for substitutes for a product.

## Views and sorting

- **List** and **Tile** views are in the list options. In tile view, the same place has **Tiles per row**: **Auto** (3 on a phone, 4 on a folding phone, 5 on a tablet) or a number you choose. A product photo fills the whole tile, edge to edge, with the name over the bottom of it. The small drawn icons keep their place above the name.
- Sort by category or alphabetically.
- **Keep screen on** stops a wall tablet from sleeping while the list is open.
