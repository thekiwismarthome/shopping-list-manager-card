# Region and catalogue

The integration ships with a product catalogue for each supported country, with prices in the local currency. Open **Settings → Region & Catalog** to choose yours.

## Supported countries

| Region | Currency |
|---|---|
| New Zealand | NZD |
| Australia | AUD |
| United States | USD |
| United Kingdom | GBP |
| Canada | CAD |
| Belgium, France and Spain | EUR (Belgium has a smaller catalogue) |

## Switching region

Switching region replaces the catalogue products with the new country's. **Products you added yourself are kept.**

## Custom regions

If your country is not listed, add a custom region with a code, a name and a currency symbol.

Want to add products to a catalogue, or contribute a new country? See [Adding countries and products](/guide/adding-products).
