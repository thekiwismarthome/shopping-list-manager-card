# Region and catalogue

The integration ships with a product catalogue for each supported country, with prices in the local currency. Open **Settings → Region & Catalog** to choose yours.

## Supported countries

| Region | Currency |
|---|---|
| New Zealand | NZD |
| Australia | AUD |
| United States | USD |
| United Kingdom | GBP |
| Canada | CAD |
| Belgium, France, Germany and Spain | EUR (Belgium has a smaller catalogue) |

## Language

When you pick Spain, France or Germany, the card shows its text in Spanish, French or German, with no internet lookup needed. Germany also uses German category names, German product names and brands (Aldi, Lidl, Rewe, Edeka and more), and Open Food Facts Germany for barcode and product lookups.

## Switching region

Switching region replaces the catalogue products with the new country's. **Products you added yourself are kept.**

## Custom regions

If your country is not listed, add a custom region with a code, a name and a currency symbol.

Want to add products to a catalogue, or contribute a new country? See [Adding countries and products](/guide/adding-products).
